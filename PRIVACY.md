# Haku privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Haku is a Chrome extension that saves job postings, tracks your applications and helps you practice for interviews.

## What Haku stores
Job details you save (title, company, location, job ID, deadline, description, link), their status, interview dates, and your practice answers. All of this is stored only in your browser, using Chrome's extension storage. Haku has no accounts and keeps no copy of it.

## What leaves your browser
- When you open prep for a job in interview, Haku sends that job's title, company and description to the Haku server, which passes them to Google's Gemini API to write practice questions. The server doesn't store them.
- Haku uses Google's Gemini API free tier. Under Google's terms, Google may use this content to improve its products.
- If you answer out loud, Chrome's speech recognition turns your voice into text. Google processes that audio; Haku only keeps the text.

## What Haku doesn't do
No analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no selling or sharing of your data with anyone else.

## Deleting your data
Remove any entry in the saved tab, or uninstall Haku to delete everything.
